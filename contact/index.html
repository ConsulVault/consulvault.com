What happens next?

  1. Initial review: We review your note and reply to schedule a focused introductory conversation.
  2. Discovery discussion: We talk through your current situation, constraints, systems, and what a good outcome looks like for your team.
  3. Practical next step: If there is a good fit, we outline a clear, appropriately scoped starting point (such as a targeted health check, readiness assessment, or implementation plan).
A good place to start

Tell us a little about what you need

There is no special format to follow. A few lines about what is happening and what you would like to improve are enough to start the conversation.

What is getting in the way?

Perhaps requests are getting lost, your service desk is difficult to manage, a cloud move is approaching, or a platform has become hard to maintain. Start wherever makes sense to you.

What tools or teams are involved?

Mention any relevant systems or people, such as Jira Service Management, Jira, Confluence, or the teams involved. It is fine if you are not sure yet.

What would you like to be different?

Share what a better situation would look like for your team or users. We can work through the details together.
