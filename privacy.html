Privacy & Data Handling

Privacy Information

How ConsulVault handles visitor information on consulvault.com and when you contact us by email.

Notice for pre-publication review

This privacy summary accurately reflects the current technical behavior of this website (static informational pages, no web forms, and no analytics or tracking cookies). It must be reviewed and approved by the company owner before public launch and is not legal advice.

1. Overview

ConsulVault operates the website at consulvault.com to provide information about our workflow improvement, IT service management, and platform consulting services. We design our website to minimize data collection.

2. Website browsing and cookies

This website is delivered as a static informational site. In its current release:

  • We do not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, heatmaps, or behavioral tracking technologies.
  • We do not set non-essential cookies or require a tracking consent banner.
  • We do not operate a registration system, user account portal, or database-backed inquiry form on this website.

Like all websites on the internet, the web hosting infrastructure that serves these pages may automatically process standard technical request metadata (such as your IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamp) strictly as needed to deliver the web pages reliably and protect the service against abuse.

3. When you contact us by email

When you choose to contact ConsulVault using the email link (saidk@consulvault.com) or by emailing us directly:

  • Your message is sent through your own email client and processed via our business email service (Google Workspace).
  • We receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your name, email address, organization, and the details of your inquiry.
  • We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss potential consulting work, and manage any resulting business relationship.
  • We do not sell your contact information or add you to automated marketing mailing lists without your consent.

4. Data retention and security

Business correspondence sent to saidk@consulvault.com is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to address your inquiry, maintain accurate business records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Your privacy inquiries

If you have questions about how your information is handled, or if you wish to request access to or deletion of correspondence you have sent to us, please contact:

Email: saidk@consulvault.com

6. Updates to this page

If we introduce an online contact form, analytics tool, or additional online services in the future, we will update this page before or at the time those features are enabled so that it always reflects our actual data practices.
