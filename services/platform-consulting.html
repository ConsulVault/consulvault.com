Platforms & Governance

Platform Consulting, Implementation, and Governance

Assess, design, configure, and govern platforms used for workflows, service management, and collaboration. Atlassian products are a strong specialization within this broader offer.

Discuss this service All services
The Challenge

Why organizations look for help in this area

Over time, work management and collaboration platforms often accumulate duplicate custom fields, inconsistent project templates, confusing permissions, and ad-hoc schemes. Without thoughtful design and governance, tools become slow, hard to administer, and frustrating for users.

Common friction points we address

  • Uncontrolled creation of projects, spaces, custom fields, and workflow schemes makes maintenance difficult.
  • Access and permission models are overly permissive or too fragmented to audit confidently.
  • Teams use the same platform in incompatible ways, preventing clear cross-team visibility.
  • Organizations need objective advice that fits their broader toolset, whether centered on Atlassian or multi-vendor environments.
Typical Scope

What an engagement can cover

Every engagement is scoped around your existing environment, team capacity, and priorities. Typical work in this area includes:

  • Platform health checks covering configuration hygiene, permission structures, scheme reuse, and administrative bottlenecks.
  • Architecture and configuration of new or restructured work management and collaboration spaces.
  • Deep specialization in Atlassian products (such as Jira, Jira Service Management, and Confluence) alongside vendor-neutral platform guidance.
  • Establishment of practical platform governance standards: ownership models, change control, and provisioning templates.
Practical Outputs

Examples of tangible deliverables

Depending on your agreed scope, engagements produce clear, maintainable artifacts and working configurations such as:

  • Platform health check report and prioritized remediation roadmap
  • Clean, reusable project, space, workflow, and permission architectures
  • Practical platform governance standards and administrator runbooks
  • Hands-on configuration cleanup and consolidated schemes

How we tailor the engagement

Whether you are deploying a platform for the first time, cleaning up years of organic growth, or establishing governance standards for internal administrators, scope is adapted to your environment.

Engagement Options
