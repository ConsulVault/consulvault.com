IT Service Management (ITSM)

Improve service request, incident, change, and knowledge practices, including the processes, roles, measures, and tools that support service delivery.

Discuss this service All services
The Challenge

Why organizations look for help in this area

IT and internal service teams frequently struggle with reactive ticket firefighting, unclear prioritization, and portals that employees bypass. Effective ITSM requires practical processes and clear roles supported by well-structured tooling, rather than heavy theoretical frameworks.

Common friction points we address

  • Service requests and incidents are mixed together, making urgent disruption response harder to manage.
  • Request catalogues are overly complex or written in internal IT jargon rather than user language.
  • Change management is either too rigid (slowing down routine updates) or too informal (causing avoidable incidents).
  • Recurring questions consume agent time because knowledge articles are disconnected from the service desk.
Typical Scope

What an engagement can cover

Every engagement is scoped around your existing environment, team capacity, and priorities. Typical work in this area includes:

  • Review and streamlining of core ITSM practices: service request management, incident management, change enablement, and problem or knowledge practices.
  • Design of clear service catalogues, triage models, escalation paths, and queue structures.
  • Definition of practical, meaningful service-level targets (SLAs) and operational reporting.
  • Alignment of ITSM processes with your service management platform (including Jira Service Management or comparable tools).
Practical Outputs

Examples of tangible deliverables

Depending on your agreed scope, engagements produce clear, maintainable artifacts and working configurations such as:

  • Structured service catalogue and request taxonomy designed around user needs
  • Documented incident, request, and change workflows with clear role responsibilities
  • Refined queue, routing, and SLA configurations in your service platform
  • Practical knowledge-base structure connected to self-service and agent resolution

How we tailor the engagement

We right-size ITSM practices to your organization maturity and team size so governance strengthens reliability without introducing unnecessary bureaucracy.
