About ConsulVault

Practical consulting and hands-on delivery for better workflows and IT services

ConsulVault helps organizations improve how work gets done by simplifying workflows, strengthening IT service operations, and making the technology behind them more reliable. It combines practical consulting with hands-on implementation, so improvements work for people, not just on paper.

Purpose & Focus

Why ConsulVault exists

Organizations invest heavily in service desks, work management platforms, and collaboration tools, yet teams still find themselves chasing status across email threads, working around confusing portals, or managing platforms that have grown brittle over time.

Too often, teams are forced to choose between high-level consulting decks that leave implementation to chance, or purely technical configuration that copies existing bottlenecks into a new tool.

ConsulVault was founded to close that gap. We combine operational process thinking with hands-on technical delivery, helping organizations simplify how work moves across teams, strengthen IT service management (ITSM), and make the platforms behind them cleaner and more reliable.

What We Focus On

Three connected layers of improvement

  • Workflows and ways of working: Clarifying intake, triage, approvals, handoffs, and ownership across teams.
  • IT service operations (ITSM): Structuring service requests, incidents, changes, queues, SLAs, and knowledge so service delivery is predictable.
  • Enabling platforms and migrations: Designing, configuring, migrating, and governing the platforms that support daily work (with deep capability in Atlassian tools alongside broader service platforms).
Working Principles

How we approach every engagement

Whether we are reviewing a single service desk or planning a multi-phase platform migration, four principles guide our work.

Processes and people before software configuration

Automating or re-platforming a broken process only makes the friction happen faster. We clarify how work should flow, who owns each step, and what users actually need before locking in tool settings.

Advice connected to hands-on delivery

Recommendations are only valuable when they survive contact with day-to-day operations. We bridge advisory work and hands-on configuration, testing, and enablement so improvements work in practice, not just on paper.

Practical, maintainable governance

Every custom script, workflow scheme, or permission rule has a maintenance cost. We favor clean, well-documented configurations and sensible guardrails that internal administrators can confidently own.

Vendor-appropriate guidance with deep Atlassian expertise

ConsulVault is built around solving workflow and service operations challenges across your environment. While we bring strong specialization in the Atlassian ecosystem (including Jira Service Management, Jira, and Confluence), our focus is always on what serves your teams best.

Direct & Accountable Delivery

Hands-on expertise tailored to your scope

When you engage ConsulVault, you work directly with an experienced practitioner focused on understanding your operational context, defining a realistic scope, and delivering clean, well-documented outcomes. We do not overpromise outcomes or lock clients into unnecessary complexity.
