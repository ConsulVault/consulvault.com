How We Work

A practical, collaborative path from diagnosis to lasting adoption

Every organization has different constraints, existing systems, and team structures. Rather than forcing a rigid methodology onto every client, we use a clear five-stage framework that adapts to the size and urgency of your challenge.

Engagement Stages

Five stages from understanding to refinement

You can engage ConsulVault for an initial diagnostic and roadmap (Stages 1 to 3) or for end-to-end implementation, migration, and team enablement (Stages 1 to 5).

Stage 01

Understand

Discuss the current situation, goals, users, constraints, and definition of success.

What happens in this stage

  • Clarify the operational challenge, affected teams, and business or service objectives.
  • Identify technical, organizational, and governance constraints early.
  • Agree on what a practical, measurable improvement looks like for stakeholders and users.
Typical outcome: Shared problem statement and agreed assessment or engagement boundaries.
Stage 02

Assess

Review processes, platform configuration, risks, and opportunities within an agreed scope.

What happens in this stage

  • Examine how requests, incidents, changes, or internal workflows currently move across teams.
  • Inspect platform configuration, permissions, project/space structure, integrations, and pain points.
  • Separate root process bottlenecks from software configuration issues.
Typical outcome: Clear diagnostic findings highlighting friction points, risks, and quick wins.
Stage 03

Plan

Present prioritized recommendations, options, assumptions, and an implementation path.

What happens in this stage

  • Outline pragmatic options (from targeted adjustments to structured redesign or migration).
  • Document key dependencies, technical assumptions, and change impact on teams.
  • Sequence recommendations into logical phases so delivery remains manageable.
Typical outcome: Actionable improvement roadmap with clear scope and priorities.
Stage 04

Improve

Deliver agreed configuration, process, migration, automation, or enablement work.

What happens in this stage

  • Configure portals, workflows, queues, schemas, permissions, or automation rules in alignment with process design.
  • Coordinate migration testing, data validation, and cutover planning where applicable.
  • Keep solutions maintainable and well-documented rather than overly complex.
Typical outcome: Working, tested improvements aligned with how your teams actually operate.
Stage 05

Adopt and refine

Support handover, learning, measurement, and continued improvement where included.

What happens in this stage

  • Provide role-appropriate guidance for administrators, service agents, project owners, and end users.
  • Establish practical governance standards so platforms stay clean after launch.
  • Review early adoption feedback and refine workflows or queues as real usage progresses.
Typical outcome: Confident internal ownership, clear documentation, and sustainable operations.
Flexible Ways to Engage

Scoped around where your team needs support

Not every challenge requires a large transformation project. Engagements are structured around three practical modes.

01 · Assess

Health check, discovery, and prioritized roadmap

Review current workflows, service management practices, or platform configuration to identify bottlenecks, risks, and practical improvement options before committing to large changes.

02 · Transform

Scoped implementation, migration, or redesign

Design and deliver agreed improvements across processes, service portals, queues, platform configuration, cloud migration, or automation, with clear testing and handover.

03 · Optimize

Ongoing advisory, governance, and enablement

Support long-term reliability through practical governance standards, administration guidance, role-based training, and iterative refinement as teams evolve.

What Makes Our Delivery Different

Designed for the people who run and use the system after handover

A platform implementation or workflow redesign only succeeds if service agents, project teams, and internal administrators can operate and evolve it comfortably after the engagement ends.

We document configuration decisions clearly, avoid fragile customization when native capabilities will do the job cleanly, and involve your internal owners throughout delivery.

What you can expect when working together

  • Plain-language findings and recommendations without unnecessary jargon.
  • Clear agreement on scope, assumptions, and boundaries before implementation begins.
  • Careful attention to permissions, governance, and change impact on active teams.
  • Structured handover documentation and role-appropriate enablement.
