How We Work

Structured from initial discovery to team adoption

Whether you start with a targeted platform health check or a full service management implementation, every engagement follows a clear, collaborative path.

Stage 01

Understand

Discuss the current situation, goals, users, constraints, and definition of success.

Stage 02

Assess

Review processes, platform configuration, risks, and opportunities within an agreed scope.

Stage 03

Plan

Present prioritized recommendations, options, assumptions, and an implementation path.

Stage 04

Improve

Deliver agreed configuration, process, migration, automation, or enablement work.

Stage 05

Adopt and refine

Support handover, learning, measurement, and continued improvement where included.

01 · Assess

Health check, discovery, and prioritized roadmap

Review current workflows, service management practices, or platform configuration to identify bottlenecks, risks, and practical improvement options before committing to large changes.

02 · Transform

Scoped implementation, migration, or redesign

Design and deliver agreed improvements across processes, service portals, queues, platform configuration, cloud migration, or automation, with clear testing and handover.

03 · Optimize

Ongoing advisory, governance, and enablement

Support long-term reliability through practical governance standards, administration guidance, role-based training, and iterative refinement as teams evolve.

Why ConsulVault

Advice connected to delivery, adoption, and sustainable improvement

Many consulting engagements end with a slide deck of recommendations that internal teams lack the time to implement, or with complex tool configurations that nobody knows how to maintain six months later.

ConsulVault focuses on practical, maintainable improvements that fit how your organization actually operates.

Our commitments on every engagement

  • Process and ways of working first: We simplify the underlying workflow before locking it into software configuration.
  • Hands-on implementation: We connect consulting advice directly to portal design, workflow configuration, migration planning, and testing.
  • Maintainable governance: We favor clean structures, sensible permissions, and clear documentation over brittle workarounds.
  • Broad perspective, deep Atlassian skill: We bring strong Atlassian specialization without treating a single vendor as the answer to every problem.
